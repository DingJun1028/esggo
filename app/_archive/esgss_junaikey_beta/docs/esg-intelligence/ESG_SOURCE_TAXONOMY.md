# ESG Source Taxonomy (v1.0) - Technical Specification

This document defines the "Source Layer" and "Signal Classification" for the ESGss AI Automation Intelligence Engine.

## Source Taxonomy Overview

The system categorizes data sources into five distinct layers (S1??謕?5) based on their authority and decision-relevance.

### S1: Global Governance

**Role**: Policy Direction, International Consensus, Regulatory Foresight.

- **Sources**: UN, UNDP, UNEP, UNFCCC, IPCC, FAO, ILO, WHO, World Bank, OECD, IISD ??SDG Knowledge Hub.
- **Signals**: Policy announcements, framework updates, roadmaps.
- **Corporate Risk**: Compliance & Market Access.

### S2: Standards & Disclosure

**Role**: Definitive Rules and Reporting Languages.

- **Sources**: ISSB (IFRS Sustainability), GRI, SASB, TCFD, TNFD, SBTi.
- **Signals**: Standard revisions, disclosure requirements, sector guidance.
- **Corporate Risk**: Greenwashing & Board Liability.

### S3: Think Tanks & Research

**Role**: Structural Insights & Systemic Risk Analysis.

- **Sources**: WEF, Stockholm Resilience Centre, SEI, SIPRI, Chatham House, MIT.
- **Signals**: Long-term trends, scenario modeling, scientific thresholds.
- **Corporate Risk**: Strategic Misalignment.

### S4: Finance & Energy Systems

**Role**: Capital Allocation & Risk Re-pricing.

- **Sources**: PRI, NGFS, IEA.
- **Signals**: Investment guidance, energy transition scenarios, risk indicators.
- **Corporate Risk**: Financing Cost & Asset Stranding.

### S5: Industry & Technology

**Role**: Operational Reality & Implementation Roadmaps.

- **Sources**: SEMI, imec, EU GENESIS Project, TSMC/ASML ESG Portals.
- **Signals**: Technology constraints, supply chain dependencies.
- **Corporate Risk**: Supply Chain Disruption & Technical Failure.
