# ESG GO Platform Strategy & Roadmap (Master Document)
**Version**: 1.0.0
**Status**: ACTIVE

## 🎯 Platform Positioning
ESG GO is an integrated platform for **Report Generation + Governance + Knowledge Insight + Actionable Matrix + Trust Verification**.

## 🏗️ Product Layers
### A. Compliance & Reporting (The Production Hub)
ESG GO platforms **Core Delivery Engine** automating "Data → Analysis → Compliance → Report".
- **Report Factory Engine**: Smart indexing, multi-standard assembly, pagination-aware generation (modularized to avoid hallucinations).
- **Compliance Control Tower**: 97 KPI matching, submission timeline tracking, assurance preparation mode.
- **Benchmark & Tasking**: Peer comparison (Top 30), Gap-to-Task conversion, automatic data backfilling.
- **5T Integration**: Evidence Vault anchoring, ZKP verification codes, full audit trails.

### B. Governance & Risk
- Taiwan Legislation: FSC Disclosure, EPA Carbon Audit, MOEA Energy Management.
- 97 KPIs Monitoring.

### C. Trust & Traceability (5T Protocol)
- **Tangible**: Visualized impact metrics.
- **Traceable**: Source origin tracking.
- **Trackable**: Lifecycle path logging.
- **Transparent**: Open algorithm verification.
- **Trustworthy**: Hash Lock & Evidence Vault immutability.

## 📊 Strategic SWOT Analysis
| Strengths | Weaknesses |
| :--- | :--- |
| Full end-to-end product depth. | Product narrative heavy, needs PRD convergence. |
| Deep Taiwan legal alignment. | Multiple docs, needs Single Source of Truth. |

| Gemini 2.0 AI leadership. | Lack of role-based experience UI. |

## 🗺️ Roadmap Optimization
### Phase 1: MVP (8 Weeks)
- Report Factory Engine (Assembly + Indexing).
- GRI/TCFD Basic Compliance Check.
- Evidence Vault Deep Integration.

### Phase 2: Professional (6 Weeks)
- Taiwan Legislation Hub (97 KPIs).
- Benchmark & Gap Analysis (Top 30 Peer Data).
- Task Matrix & Auto-backfilling.

### Phase 3: Enterprise (4 Weeks)
- Assurance Mode & External Auditor Access.
- Full Blockchain Audit Chain.
- Optimized Role-based Dashboards.

## 👥 Role-Based Experience
- **CSO/ESG Manager**: Control tower, report approval, assurance packages.
- **Data Specialist**: Task execution, gap filling, quality assurance.
- **CEO/Board**: High-level risk dashboards, benchmark insights.
- **External Auditor**: 5T chain verification, trace evidence access.
